ABOUT ONE KIRAANA

India ka pehla private labeller,
kiraana stores ke liye.

Hum mandi ke andar ke log hain — 3 peedhi, 60+ saal APMC Vashi mein. Ab wahi samajh, wahi network, chhoti-medium dukaanon ke kaam aa raha hai: unke apne brand banane mein.

VISION
Traditional grocery retail mein nayi jaan — dukaan wahi, soch nayi.
Revolutionizing traditional grocery retail.
MISSION
Collaboration se lasting impact — dukaandaar ke saath, dukaandaar ke liye.
Lasting impact through collaboration.
Team One Kiraana — young, wise aur driven.
लोकल दुकान बनाम ग्लोबल ब्रांड
"Quick commerce aur bade brands ke zamaane mein, mohalle ki dukaan ki sabse badi taakat uska apna naam hai. Customer aap pe bharosa karta hai — brand pe nahi. Wahi bharosa, ab pack pe."

Kiraana stores ko replace karne ki zaroorat nahi — unhe infrastructure chahiye. Sourcing, cleaning, packing, branding: jo cheez sirf bade brands ke paas thi, ab aapki dukaan ke paas bhi hai. Bina kisi direct ya indirect khatre ke — sirf empowerment.
